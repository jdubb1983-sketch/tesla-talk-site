Tesla Talk

Tesla Talk is a YouTube channel with a short briefing every weekday on Tesla news, software updates and technology: what changed, who gets it, and what it means for owners.

Episodes are researched from public news sources, written with AI assistance, fact-checked, and narrated with a synthetic voice. The channel owner reviews and approves every episode before it is published.

About the Tesla Talk uploader

The Tesla Talk uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload finished episodes, add captions and playlists to the owner's own channel, and publish them after the owner approves. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else and does not access any other person's data.
